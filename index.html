The challenge

Put your ideas into action.

The AI-Powered Autonomous Security Challenge 2027 invites students to explore an emerging area where artificial intelligence meets autonomous security. Bring your curiosity, think creatively and be part of a competition built around ambitious ideas.
Teams will be challenged to develop a solution that uses AI and autonomous penetration testing against a custom-written PHP web application. The competition is looking for an innovative, offline solution that better emulates real-world situations where clients might not want any interaction with online AI. The solution should function without relying on online AI services and require as little user interaction as possible: give it a target and let it do its magic.

Take part

Competition rules

Here are the rules and guidelines for participating in the competition.

Eligibility

All Roehampton students and recent graduates are eligible to participate in the competition. Each team must include at least one STEM student. Any questions regarding eligibility should be directed to Liam Harcourt.

Challenge guidelines

This challenge is designed to encourage students to explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, autonomous security and entrepreneurship. Teams are encouraged to think creatively and develop innovative solutions, sharing their ideas with the community.

Submissions and judging

Submissions will be judged by a panel of experts based on criteria such as innovation, feasibility and accuracy. A full list of judging criteria will be provided to all participants at the kick-off event.
